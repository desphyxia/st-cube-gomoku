How to play

  • The board is the whole surface of the cube — six faces of N×N tiles. Drag to spin it, scroll to zoom.
  • Players alternate claiming empty tiles. First to five in a row wins.
  • A row does not stop at the edge of a face: it rolls over onto the next face and keeps going straight. Straight rows circle the whole cube in 4N tiles.
  • Diagonals wrap over edges too, but they stop at the cube's eight corners, where only 270° of surface meet.
  • On a small cube there is no five-in-a-row on a single face — every win has to travel around the cube.
  • Watch every angle. The threat that beats you is usually on the face you cannot see.
